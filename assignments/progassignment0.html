Programming Assignment 0: Getting Started

Goal: Become familiar with Processing’s coordinate system and practice placing shapes precisely on the canvas.

Your Task

Create a new Processing sketch. Your sketch must contain:

  • two diagonal lines: one from the upper-left corner to the lower-right corner and one from the upper-right corner to the lower-left corner;
  • a rectangle 200 pixels wide and 300 pixels high, with its upper-left corner at (0, 0);
  • a circle with diameter 100, centered on the canvas;
  • several additional shapes of your choice.

Experiment with:

  • stroke() and fill();
  • color and transparency (alpha values);
  • different shapes, such as ellipse(), triangle(), and quad().

Add comments identifying the two diagonal lines, the rectangle, and the centered circle. Be creative, but make sure all required elements are clearly visible and easy to identify.

Challenge

Change the dimensions of your canvas in size(). Does the circle remain centered? If not, revise the code so that it does. The built-in variables width and height will help.

Submission

Upload your properly named and compressed sketch to Brightspace.
