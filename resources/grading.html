How Grading Works in CS1

This course uses a proficiency-based grading system designed to prioritize learning, revision, and conceptual understanding over point-chasing.

Levels

Level 3: Proficient

You have demonstrated proficiency with the material for this assignment.

Level 2: Progressing

You are close, but the work needs refinement.

To upgrade to Level 3

  • Revise your work based on the feedback.
  • Submit an improved version within one week of receiving feedback.
  • Be prepared to explain your revisions if asked.
Level 1: Developing

The work is incomplete or needs significant correction.

To upgrade your level

  • Come to office hours or schedule a short meeting.
  • Revise your work based on the feedback.
  • Submit an improved version within one week of receiving feedback.
Level 0: No submission

Nothing was submitted for evaluation.

Next steps

  • Start the assignment as soon as possible.
  • Come to office hours early; this course is cumulative.

Revision and Late Submission Policy

Programming is cumulative: falling behind makes later topics harder. At the same time, unlimited late work becomes unmanageable. This policy is designed to balance both.

Deadlines

  • Assignments are due on the posted date and time.
  • Each student has two late passes during the semester.
  • Each late pass extends the deadline by seven days.
  • To use a late pass, email me before the deadline.

Revisions

  • Level 1 and Level 2 work may be revised within one week of receiving feedback.
  • Revisions must address the feedback.
  • I may ask you to explain your revisions during office hours.

Final limits

No work is accepted after the course has moved beyond the assignment or after the end-of-semester deadline.
